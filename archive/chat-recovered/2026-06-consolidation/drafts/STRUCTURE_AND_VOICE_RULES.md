## The Structure

**Preface: The Warrior and the Weight**
Opens with the Dominican Republic trip -- the zipline stairs, the banana boat, swimming to shore at 270 lbs. The moment you knew something had to change. Sets the tone for everything that follows.

**Ground Book (Chapters 1-4)**
Ch 1: Know the Ground -- the segmentation model failure story
Ch 2: Stated vs. Real Problem -- the churn analysis / smart speaker story
Ch 3: Choose the Right Tool -- the ColdFusion ETL $150K emergency
Ch 4: Build on Rock -- foundational practices, no single story anchor

**Water Book (Chapters 5-8)**
Ch 5: Adapt or Die -- the NN-to-XGBoost pivot
Ch 6: Flow State -- the 1 AM in-the-zone story
Ch 7: The 80% Solution -- knowing when done is better than perfect
Ch 8: Rhythm and Recovery -- sustainability over heroics

**Fire Book (Chapters 9-12)**
Ch 9: Win the Room -- the $50M product launch delay recommendation
Ch 10: When They Say No -- two resistance stories (inventory + call center)
Ch 11: Build Capital -- how to earn the right to be heard
Ch 12: Know When to Walk Away -- the chapter a textbook would never write

**Wind Book (Chapter 13)**
Ch 13: Failure Patterns -- four chapters collapsed into one. How data projects and careers actually die.

**Void Book (Chapters 14-17)**
Ch 14: See the Invisible
Ch 15: Recognizing Dysfunction
Ch 16: The Long Game
Ch 17: The Path Forward -- closes with final legacy reflection, your tenth story

## Voice Rules (Non-Negotiable)

First person throughout. Summary before detail. Dry wit is allowed. Zero corporate vocabulary. No em dashes. Short sentences carry the weight. Every chapter opens with a Musashi quote and closes with five Sword Drills. 22 total Musashi quotes across the book. Sun Tzu: cut entirely. One tradition.

No chapter templates. "Why X Matters" and "Core Principles of X" are dead. Chapter shape: quote, story that earns the point, the argument in your voice, Sword Drills.
