# Proposed manuscript architecture

**Working title:** The Data Warrior's Manuscript

**Working subtitle:** The Way of Strategy, Mastery, and Intelligent Action in the Age of Data and AI

**Central thesis:** Mastery is disciplined understanding, strategic choice, responsible execution, critical examination, and continuous learning—not mastery of technology alone.

## Recurring chapter pattern
Principle; modern challenge; substantive teaching; Council perspectives; Sword Drill; Living Scroll update; historically accurate Musashi parallel.

## Living Scroll
The Scroll is the active challenge workspace containing challenge, terrain, Battle Map, Council, Forge, examination, and Chronicle.

## Source fidelity
Musashi's original five books are distinct from modern extensions. Historical quotes must be verified and attributed; interpretations labeled as such.

## Migration
Preserve original drafts intact. Build a chapter-level crosswalk showing keep/revise/merge/retire, with citations to source draft sections before generating replacement prose.
