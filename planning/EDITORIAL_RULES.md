# Editorial rules

- The first five rings are Musashi’s Earth, Water, Fire, Wind and Void; People, Tools and Practice are contemporary additions.
- Name the Council roles Strategist, Architect, Engineer, Guardian, Examiner and Sensei.
- The Living Scroll holds the challenge, terrain, Battle Map, Council contributions, Forge output, examination and Chronicle, not just generated documents.
- Preserve author-supplied stories verbatim, separately from AI prose; flag unverified December 2025 Preface signoff and manufacturer supply-chain anecdote.
- Verify attributed Musashi quotations against a named edition or clearly label as paraphrase.
- Treat original DOCX and chat-recovered material as immutable; all rewriting goes into chapter working files.
- Retain meaningful draft alternatives and log merge/retire decisions.
- Do not invent citations, case-study specifics, chapter completion or approval.
