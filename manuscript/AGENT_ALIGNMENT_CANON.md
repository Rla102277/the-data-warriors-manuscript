**Source of agent truth:** [The Data Warrior agent](https://github.com/Rla102277/The-data-warrior-agent), especially `THE_WAY.md`, `SCOPE.md`, `README.md`, and `SCROLL.md`, inspected 2026-10-19. This document is a **manuscript editorial decision proposal**, not a change to the agent or an assertion that the existing reading draft already complies.

## Thesis

**Technical completion is not strategic success.** The human is the Data Warrior. AI augments judgment and cannot grant itself authority. Analytics is service: the value is a decision changed, waste avoided, trust repaired or risk exposed—not artifacts delivered.

## The two demands

1. **Demand simplicity.** Identify the single action a decision-maker can take now, in one sentence. Refuse to build without knowing what decision matters.
2. **Embrace discipline.** Ground the claim, specify evidence, implement the smallest reversible step, test it for real, independently review, obtain human approval, observe the result, and learn.

## The Forge is a consequence, not the protagonist

The Forge is the software factory in the agent repository. It operates only after a documented ground gate. Its workflow specifies the action, decomposes it into small steps, implements one proven step, records actual test execution and independent security/naming/data-quality reviews, and **stops for human approval**. The `--authorized` flag is an operator attestation, not cryptographic identity. The manuscript must not claim that CI structure checks prove behavioral success, or that AI can approve its own work.

Do not copy Foundation estate knowledge into the book or agent. Foundation is a separate workplace platform. The book must stand alone for readers who never install the agent.

## Narrative revision rules

- Rebuild the 36-chapter edition around the five-ring journey first. Retain People, Tools and Practice as explicitly modern extensions; avoid representing eight rings as Musashi's historical structure.
- Recover and prioritize the December 2025 five-book prose, especially simplicity, discipline, 80% shipping, winning the decision, ten failure patterns, power/incentives/culture and knowing when to walk away. These are source candidates, not verified autobiography.
- Keep the author's first-person stories only where source-supported and fact-checked. Never invent client names, business outcomes, quotations or precise metrics.
- End each chapter with a substantive Sword Drill that produces evidence, a decision, or an observable behavior; not a generic reflection prompt.
- Explain agent command names sparingly, in a companion field guide or crosswalk; main chapters must read as a real book without requiring the software.
- Make the Council roles distinct from collaboration modes: Strategist/Architect/Engineer/Guardian/Examiner/Sensei are review perspectives; Deshi/Rōnin/Kensei are user-agent working styles and never confer permissions.
- Editorially assess every prior chapter against this doctrine before accepting it. Repetition, generic cloud-platform catalogs and ungrounded examples are not sufficient prose.

## Completion definition

A **full draft** is continuous chapter prose that develops a specific thesis with examples, objections, transitions, and exercises across the entire book, rather than 36 brief templated summaries. A **final draft** also requires consistent voice, historical and statistical fact-checking, anecdote verification, substantive line editing and author review. The existing 45,629-word reading draft is neither automatically aligned nor publication-ready merely because every chapter exists.
