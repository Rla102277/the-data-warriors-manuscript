# Manuscript migration and preservation plan

## Phase 1 — Recover
Acquire actual DOCX files. Archive byte-for-byte; calculate hashes. Never call a conversation excerpt an original.

## Phase 2 — Inventory
Extract all headings, paragraphs, tables, images, quotations, references, and drills. Produce a source-indexed chapter inventory.

## Phase 3 — Reconcile
Compare overlapping drafts and choose a canonical starting draft only after reviewing their differences. Build source-to-destination mapping for each chapter.

## Phase 4 — Rewrite
Create chapter Markdown under manuscript/ grouped by book. Keep narrative, verified Musashi parallels, Council perspectives, Sword Drills, and Living Scroll progress.

## Phase 5 — Quality
Check quotation provenance, continuity, repetitions, style, historical fidelity, source coverage, and narrative coherence.

## Phase 6 — Publication
Generate DOCX/PDF/EPUB from source without changing archive originals. Track review status in PROJECT_STATE.md.

## Tool handoff
Every editing session reads AGENTS.md and PROJECT_STATE.md, then records decisions, changed files, and next steps before committing.
