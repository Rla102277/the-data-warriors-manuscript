# The Data Warrior: Full Project Summary

## What This Book Is

A 180-200 page practitioner's philosophy for working data professionals. Not a textbook. Not a consulting playbook. A career guide written by someone who has 24 years of scar tissue and knows exactly where the bodies are buried. The Miyamoto Musashi Five Rings framework is the skeleton, but your voice and your ten personal stories are the muscle.

## Voice Rules (Non-Negotiable)

First person throughout. Summary before detail. Dry wit is allowed. Zero corporate vocabulary. No em dashes. Short sentences carry the weight. Every chapter opens with a Musashi quote and closes with five Sword Drills. 22 total Musashi quotes across the book. Sun Tzu: cut entirely. One tradition.

No chapter templates. "Why X Matters" and "Core Principles of X" are dead. Chapter shape: quote, story that earns the point, the argument in your voice, Sword Drills.
