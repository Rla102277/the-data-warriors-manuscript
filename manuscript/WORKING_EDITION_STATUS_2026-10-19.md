## Editorial decisions

1. GitHub's `manuscript/` directory is the intended clean manuscript, not the raw-source archive.
2. Preserve original text where source passages are used; keep source attribution in a separate provenance manifest.
3. Treat the prior 77K-word automatic reconstruction as research, not a finished book.
4. Never label automated source selection as final editorial judgment.
5. Do not silently fabricate author anecdotes, historical quotations or case-study statistics.
